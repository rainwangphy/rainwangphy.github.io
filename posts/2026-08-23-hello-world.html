Hello, world

  • meta

Papers are the wrong container for a lot of what I think about: half-formed research directions, negative results, reading notes, and advice I end up repeating to students one at a time. This blog is where those go.

What I plan to write about

  • Research notes — single- and multi-agent reinforcement learning, game-theoretic solvers, and world models.
  • Foundation agents — what actually works when you try to make a language model act, and what quietly does not.
  • AI for science — mostly materials, mostly lessons learned the slow way.
  • Doing research — notes for students on picking problems, writing, and reviewing.

How this works

Each post is a plain HTML file under posts/. There is no build step and no comment section — if you want to discuss something here, please email me.
